Application Description: 
"We need to develop a book club discussion room application.  On a scheduled day people meet to discuss a book. General users can view the comments posted in the room. Those who have read the book and want to participate actively can register to post comments. Registered users can request permission to post comments. Registered users are given turns by the moderator. One can only post comment when it is their turn – or pass. They can also view the comments – including who posted them." - Iyengar S.
